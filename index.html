Hello, I’m Bingyang.

I study how reinforcement learning can improve sequential decisions in engineering systems. I earned my PhD at China University of Geosciences (Wuhan), where I was advised by Prof. Zhi Zhong.

Research

I combine deep reinforcement learning and surrogate modeling to optimize hydraulic fracturing treatments and predict reservoir production.

Previously

I was a visiting PhD researcher at the University of Alberta from September 2024 to February 2026, hosted by Prof. Jeff Boisvert.
