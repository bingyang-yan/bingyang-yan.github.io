About

Bingyang Yan received his PhD from China University of Geosciences in June 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Zhi Zhong. From Sep 2024 to Feb 2026, he was a visiting PhD student at the University of Alberta, working with Prof. Jeff Boisvert.

Research

His research focused on model-free reinforcement learning for optimal control in complex engineering systems, particularly those involving high-dimensional spatiotemporal dynamics and computationally expensive simulations. His expertise lies in modeling these dynamics and developing learned surrogate environments to accelerate policy learning. By combining physics-based simulation, surrogate modeling, and policy optimization, he addressed computational bottlenecks in continuous control and engineering decision-making. He implemented these methods primarily in Python and PyTorch.

Interests

  • Multi-Agent RL, Hierarchical RL, Safe RL, Model-Based RL (World Models, MPC)
  • RL with GNNs and Transformers
  • Agentic RL, LLM (Post-Training), VLM
  • Intelligent Decision-Making and Optimal Control in Engineering and Industrial Systems
